# Athenaeum specification — changelog

One version, four parts (Part I §8). This file is the amendment index: what each version changed and why, compressed. The full story of any change — the measurements, the migration sweeps, the rejected alternatives — lives in git history (`git log -- spec/`) and under tags; the ruling trail lives in the tracker. The spec text itself carries current law only.

## The unified line

- **v32** (2026-08-21, owner ruling) — **The payload-identity principle.** Born from a live strand: the v31 worked example's legacy migration held on roster-vs-streamed hash mismatches — seven promoted audio leaves whose single-track-container bytes, muxed under `-c copy -fflags +bitexact` (the 3.12 pinned-invocation regime), could no longer be reproduced after an ffmpeg upgrade; byte length identical, hash moved, records stranded. The ruling: **identity keys on the fully-unwrapped payload; wrappers are residence forms** (Part II §2). A member's identity bytes are the content with every wrapper removed — a media track's raw elementary stream in decode order (the container's own sample tables, concatenated), a compressed member's decompressed bytes — because *unwrapping* is a pure engine-free function of container bytes while *wrapping* is engine work that drifts across tool versions even pinned. Consequences, each normative: (1) the **identity equation** — `corpus://<leaf> ≡ corpus://<container>?<address>`, checkable by re-derivation, making §2's "every route yields identical bytes" a verifiable invariant; (2) **route unification** (§6.2) — a leaf resolves *through* its container, `corpus://<leaf>?<ops>` and `corpus://<container>?stream_id=<n>&<ops>` are one resolution, one cache entry, with the base form disclosed on leaf resolution; every playable/framed rendering is a derived version-labeled surface where engine drift is harmless; terminal `stream_id=` yields the payload; (3) the **`framing:` stamp retires** (§7.1) — no engine in the identity path means no producer to attest; the leaf MAY attest `samples:` directly; (4) the **single-track media container prohibition lifts** (§1.2) — payload identity dissolves the second-identity objection, so it is an ordinary one-row manifest, its wrapper retained for its tables; (5) **compressed single-member envelopes collapse at ingest** — the record minted is the payload's, the envelope hash attested as delivery provenance (§12.3.13 precedent), so the same content wrapped or bare mints the same record; (6) the §11 deferred item narrows from "deterministic stream extraction" (solved by construction) to Matroska/EBML reader coverage; §12.8's sample-sequence comparison becomes the migration's own exactness proof (old muxed leaf → payload leaf: byte-different, sample-identical). Migration: deliberate, fleet-wide, once — promoted-leaf members re-attest to payload hashes, leaves re-mint, rosters re-point (`ath ledger supersede`, whose roster rows rewrite unconditionally as of the v31 follow-up sweep); after it, re-derivation is id-stable forever. Migrating an *existing* envelope record (as opposed to ingest-time collapse, which stamps the capture origin on the payload directly) must also **transfer the envelope's producer origin onto the payload record** before the envelope is removed — a collapsed payload whose only origin block is containment lineage into a removed envelope is dangling lineage (`corpus health` flags it; coverage refuses to materialize the nonexistent target), learned live on the first migrated instance. **Named direction, not yet law**: store-wrapping — the artifact store holding bytes compressed or encrypted at rest under the same payload identity (the invariant becoming "decodes to the id'd bytes") — deferred to its own amendment on this foundation.

- **v31** (2026-08-21, owner ruling) — **The manifestation tier and the sweep declaration.** Two coupled growths, both born from one design ruling: the ontology stays split by nature — byte-shape law in the corpus, world-meaning in the ledger — and the discordance between them closes by *channel*, not by inversion. (1) **The representation layer** (Part III §4.2, §9, §4.4): the artifact roster is named as the **manifestation tier** — the concept is the work, each rostered record one representation of it; the relation is universal in principle, materialized on demand, and the record-shadow prohibition stands untouched. Roster entries gain four optional fields: `modality` (registered carrier vocabulary), `expression` (a version-grouping slug — a cut, an edition — promoted to a concept only when claims target the version itself), and `derived_from` + `derivation` (item-grain mechanical sameness — encode-of, remux-of — stated at the roster, never as a concept). Coverage (§9) gains **representation demand**: each uncovered record's backlog entry carries the prescribed work item (identify the work, stub it if new, roster the manifestation) — drainable frontier with a total horizon (eventually every in-scope record rostered or cited) on a demand-driven schedule; the reverse read (rostered-but-never-cited manifestations, per concept) lands beside it. Type schemas (§4.4) gain **`normalization_intent:`** — prose the citation discipline's enqueue carries down through the queue's `--hint` seam composed with the graph's own knowledge (participant identities as codebook lexicons), under proposes/disposes: the ledger proposes, the normalizer disposes against the bytes. Codebook positions become **durable** (Part II §7.8): a split appends and re-points, the conflated entry stays occupied and unreusable (the §4.1 lineage discipline at record scale), and re-pointing passes emit their migration worklist loudly. (2) **The sweep declaration** (Part II §4.3.3.6): the annotations zone's second namespace — `issue`'s positive twin. A `sweep` context block declares one extraction class (`text/ocr`, `text/transcript`, `structural`) carried to **exhaustion** over a band of the record's address space: inside the band, absence of a segment means absence of content; outside every sweep, extraction stays **sparse by default** and the bytes remain the band's representation. Fail-closed and earned (only the pass that processed the whole band may write one; `detector` + provenance per the issue mold), never an assertion of rendering (markers keep their meaning; formed spans need no sweep), and meaningful when empty (*no on-screen text in this range*). Same-kind sweeps never overlap; a widened re-sweep replaces its band. Sweep-backed **negative evidence** (citing an absence) is the named extension point in Part III §6. Spec-hygiene rider: worked examples carry generic placeholder identities only — the real addresses and operator paths the text carried are replaced. Migration: zero mandatory — no record carries a sweep (sparse is today's reading, now stated), and every existing roster entry remains complete as-is.

- **v30** (2026-08-21, owner ruling) — **The open disclosure set and the one-hop grammar.** Two coupled growths. (1) **Tenancy tiers** (Part III §6.4): the `public | private` binary generalizes to an instance-declared open set in the value-kinds mold — `public` (publishable) and `private` (the floor, never grantable) stay reserved with fixed semantics; further tiers (`family`, `accountant`, …) are declared in `athenaeum.yaml` (Part I §2.3) beside named **audiences** (audience → grant set of tiers; `public` implicit in every audience). Record tenancy becomes a derived **union** of origin-declared tiers (generalizing any-public-wins: capture only widens); visibility is grant intersection; **private-backed** is redefined as *not visible to {public}*, so the §12 publication wall's language survives verbatim; claim-level assertion stays the binary upward-only `sensitivity: private` (per-claim tier narrowing deliberately not grammar). The read surface (Part I §5.1) gains an **audience plane per declared audience**, token-gated like the owner plane (tokens deployment state, never tracked) — scoped disclosure as a projection, not a copy; interpretations remain owner-plane-only. The v28 "per-audience grants" out-of-scope entry retires as intended, replaced by "per-consumer/per-request grants" (disclosure per declared audience, never negotiated at the door). Migration: an instance declaring no `tenancy:` block has exactly the pre-v30 binary, byte-identically — zero mandatory migration. (2) **Demand-condition growth** (Part III §14, from live specimen #205 — appointment-site-address firing on the visiting subject): conditions gain **`id:`** (operator grammar over the fact's id, `equals`/`in` lineage-resolved; identity exclusion as the perspective idiom) and **`related:`** — one-hop cross-fact conditions over the fact-reaching §12.1 traversal kinds (`via: object|entity|wikilink|edge` — `roster` excluded as fact-free, malformed rather than vacuous; optional `edge:` selector, nested `where:` in the same grammar with `related:` barred from nesting, `exists: true|false`, missing-is-false). One hop is deliberate — depth stays scope's business; closure-level conditions and aggregates remain the named extension point.

- **v29** (2026-08-21, owner ruling) — **Named expectation ids.** First-live-test corrective to v28's demand layer: blocking a schema-expectation demand requires a **stable name** — an expectation entry MAY declare `id:` (Part III §4.4), registered in the demand-rule namespace (unique across `demands/` rules and named expectations, §13.1); a `needs` entry's `demand:` names a declared rule or a named expectation, and the positional display ids (`expectation:{type}[{i}]`) are display-only and never blockable — the v28 evaluator honored them for blocking while check rejected them, and they silently renumber under schema-list reordering, so nothing durable may reference one; validation's rejection now points at the one-line fix. Landed with the live test's conformance/ergonomics sweep: the demand surface renders the promised answer shapes (§14 "with their shapes attached"), satisfied demands name their satisfying claim, fully-satisfied evaluations say so, and the work-list frontier lists each demand-covered item once.

- **v28** (2026-08-20, owner ruling) — **The doorway, the types, the demands.** Three coupled additions. (1) **The read surface** (Part I §5.1): `ath serve` — the product's one network doorway, read-only, contract-not-framework (resource model + scope grammar + plane semantics + a spec-version-stamped OpenAPI document; the framework is unnamed implementation detail). Two planes: the **public plane** (default) serves the public projection — sensitivity-filtered fail closed in system code, so a publishing consumer that reads only it discharges the §12 wall by construction; the **owner plane** (explicit, token-gated) serves the full instance, interpretations included. Localhost by default; deployment posture is the operator's; "a network serving protocol" and the standalone `ledger://` resolver leave Part I §9's out-of-scope list, replaced by federation, surface search, surface writes, and per-audience grants. (2) **Value kinds** (Part III §4.5): instance-declared typed-value shapes (`schemas/values/{kind}.yaml`) over a **closed primitive-constraint vocabulary** (`decimal`, `enum`, `pattern`, `range`, `iso-instant`, `iana-zone`, `iso-4217`, `latitude`/`longitude`, …) — the mechanics shipped, the ontology instance-owned; shipped kinds (`money`, `instant`, `coordinate`, …) are adoptable import templates, never law; kinds register in `VOCAB.md`; typing an existing field emits a migration worklist; kinds are data — the validator-code seam is designed but deferred (Part I §9). **Scope selection and traversal** is specified once (Part III §12.1: seed / traverse / close, operator grammar, lineage-resolved, deterministic closure). (3) **Demands** (Part III §14): the completeness third of ledger integrity (grounding §13.2, coherence §11) — declared rules for what asserted content owes, `expectations:` absorbed as type-local sugar, conditions fact-local with the cross-fact extension point named. Filing is never denied: demands are frontier (open / satisfied / **blocked**), never errors; a blocked demand is an interpretation `needs` entry naming its rule (`demand:`) and *is* ingestion pressure — demand-pull pointed at acquisition. Landed with zero mandatory instance migration: nothing is typed, no rule exists, and the surface is additive until declared/run.

- **v27** (2026-08-20, owner ruling) — **One corpus-local code tier.** The corpus-local `capturers/` and `drafters/` seams retire; **shapers** (`<corpus_root>/shapers/*.py`, Part II §12.4.3) become the sole corpus-local code tier, and the loader-contract text (path import, idempotency, trust boundary) moves there. `capture.capturer:` closes over the shipped capturers (`browser` | `video`); the draft stage runs the package's generic drafters only (the html sub-drafter hook is removed with its one registration path). Owner-directed alongside the instance's retirement of its own corpus-local code: the retired iMessage drafter's output remains, in git history, the byte-level contract a future shaper must reproduce — records drafted under it are grandfathered, and a re-derive before that shaper exists degrades to the generic single-segment draft (the instance's imessage runbook carries the note). `corpus init` no longer scaffolds `capturers/`.

- **v26** (2026-08-19, owner ruling) — **The topology inversion.** The orchestrator-repo-as-workspace model retires: the distribution (spec + tooling) points at the **instance** — one private repository merging the former corpus and ledger member repos, both histories preserved, behind a sensitivity- and verification-invariance gate. The member manifest becomes the tracked instance config (`athenaeum.yaml` at the instance root; the reference-dataset registry gains git history); `ledger.yaml` and the member-sync machinery (`ath sync`) dissolve; multi-corpus plumbing is removed as unexercised. **Part IV (Custody)** is extracted from Part II's location/store/scanner material (§12.1.1, §12.9.2 become pointer stubs). Amendment archaeology moves out of the spec text into this file; Parts II and III are rewritten in current-form voice with section numbering preserved.

## The closed ATH-CORPUS line (≤3.14)

The corpus contract's own version line, closed at the v15 unification. Highlights; the numbered migration narratives (the former §12.16–12.37) are in git history (`spec/corpus-history.md` at its deletion commit, and the `pre-reforge` tag for older material).

- **3.14** — the deferral principle as deployment law: normalization runs only under demand; no standing drain, no pre-emptive forming sweeps (Part II §8.5).
- **3.13** (2026-07-30, #124) — the `frame=` axis for animated images: ordinal selection, inclusive-span assertions, `address-frame-invalid` gating against the attested `frame_count`.
- **3.12** (2026-08-07, #153) — **the freeze**: the record grammar's declared final shape; amendments land complete (text + enforcement sweep + migration) or not at all. Promoted stream leaves become single-track containers of the source's family (`framing:` stamp attests the muxer into the identity path); single-track media containers are `work`, judged from containment lineage; 3.11's timeline inheritance retires.
- **3.11** — `cut_strategy` / the `cutting:` stamp (where a timeline is cut, resolved once at promotion, held on count drift); the `scene` introspection op.
- **3.10** — `whole_address` admissibility (`admissible` / `forbidden` / `single_unit_only` against an attested count): whether an address-less segment may name the whole transport is a declared property of the medium.
- **3.9** — regions nest (innermost declaration governs); the `el=` limit recorded: text in no element has no tight address — the remedy is one segment at the container, at the record layer.
- **3.8** — the **placement** segment (member positioning; placement obliges promotion; deconstructed import match+exhaustive constraints); the structural mark's text moves to the segment body; `address` becomes optional (absence names the whole transport); origin `regions:` and `exemplars:` declarations; normalization pressure as the second demand source.
- **3.7** — the form span's envelope is derived, never stored (the section `address` field retires; the whole-record section stops being spellable).
- **3.6** — the `el=` space becomes **total** (every element addressable; no whitelist to rot); parser identity + element count attested.
- **3.5** — **the faithfulness amendment**: descriptive content has no home in a record — segment/member/issue descriptions, editorial header fields, the frontmatter title/description override, and the `reference`/`relation` context namespaces all retire; markers say where, never what; summary is a ledger query. Editorial identity is derived-only (artifact → origin role marks). `canonical:` retires outright.
- **3.4** — the **members block** (one unabridged four-key roster replaces per-asset embed blocks; descriptors move to the `members` derivation); raw-blake3/functional-URI wikilink forms retire from bodies; cross-reference resolution becomes a read-time view.
- **3.3** — **terminal contracts** (`form/passthrough`, `form/manifest`): formless-by-design made assertable; governing-contract precedence; editorial templates (origin layer); the mailbox chrome strip, window reduction, year-split, and temporal stratification standard.
- **3.2** — derived editorial fields: display title/description computed from role-marked fields, never stored; the authored record state dissolves into the form layer.
- **3.1** — `status` retires with the stub/normalized lifecycle: a record is **born complete** (the artifact's proxy); formless is the zeroth form; the queue is standing demand, never a backlog.
- **3.0** — the **draft stage retires** (attestation + derivation ops + normalize); the `form` namespace (shape contracts, minting test, generic shapes); `disposition:` (manifest/work); structural segments as byte-marks; the muxing contract; unit ops (`turn=`); default-member resolution.
- **2.1** — `artifact_kind` removed (nothing explodes at ingest); containment + promotion (members resolvable through containers, the member index); pre-ingest bundle assembly.
- **2.0** — **classification to the ledger**: the composite namespace, classify blocks, concept namespace, and the local KB retire; deterministic membership becomes ledger harvest; the corpus asserts nothing about meaning.

## The closed ATH-LEDGER line (≤1.9)

- **1.9** — evidence sensitivity re-keys from corpus membership to **record tenancy** (origin-overlay `tenancy:` declarations, lineage inheritance, any-public-wins) — the amendment the v14 single-corpus consolidation ran behind.
- **1.8** — **deferred surfaces graded, not gated**: evidence citing a `segments`-surface record with no persisted segments is admissible at every rung but excluded from the confirmed bar; the citation itself is the normalize demand (cite-then-pressure; 1.4's enqueue-before-cite retires).
- **1.7** — the **lineage map** (`facts/LINEAGE.json`) replaces per-file redirect tombstones; claim-id lineage through merges (shorts preserved, collisions renamed loudly with a migration worklist).
- **1.6** — the citable-surface contract reconciled against corpus 3.4–3.12 (folds #70): citable surfaces are the faithful rendering and attested byte-facts; retired descriptive fields read tolerantly, never cited fresh.
- **1.5** — the derivation-op version pin settles: the sources-entry `verified` binding gains the `ops` map; verification resolves derived-surface anchors through the corpus resolver.
- **1.4** — per-mime `citation_surface`: citing a segments-surface record without persisted segments became an error (superseded by 1.8's grading); interpretations exempt.
- **1.3** — terminal-contract records are **complete sources**: full-strength evidence; the normalize-demand signal narrows to formless-for-now; member needs are `promote`, never "normalize the container".
- **1.2** — the authored-state amendment tracked: citable authored prose narrows to body content under an authoring touch, never the frontmatter pair.
- **1.1** — citability re-keys from record status to **verifiable surfaces**; the snapshot binding gains derivation-op pins.
- **1.0** (2026-07-02) — the ledger layer lands: concepts/edges/claims/evidence, the epistemic ladder, the authentication bar, interpretations, harvest, invariants, coverage, `ledger://`, the check/verify contract.
